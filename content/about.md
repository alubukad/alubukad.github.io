Hi!

My name is Cem Özden, As there are many Cem Özden's out in this world, I go ahead with the nickname `alubukad` on the internet. `alubukad` is a [portmanteau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portmanteau) meaning it's formed by concatenating the names of anime characters [Alucard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alucard_(Hellsing)), [Majin Buu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Majin_Buu) and [Katara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katara_(Avatar:_The_Last_Airbender)) (`t` is softened.)

As you could already figure out, I used to be all-in anime kid when i was growing. Though, I turned my anime passion into software development not so long after. I've been pretty much in software development world since I was going to the secondary school.

I've been mainly involved in developing backend solutions, distributed systems in my professinal career using `Java`, `Scala`, `TypeScript` while I maintain interests in Rust Programming Language, Linux, Cloud/OnPrem Infrastructure.

Outside of the IT world, I'm a big fan of Formula 1 and have been interested in doing podcasts about technical side of F1. Unfortunately, I don't have the time and energy to pursue doing podcasting anymore. (Maybe one day!)

I currently live in `Amsterdam, The Netherlands` and working for a large financial institution in Utrecht. Previously, I lived in Copenhagen, Kraków and Budapest/Debrecen and originally from Izmir/Turkey. I wrote a blog post about my experience living abroad. You can find it [here](/blog/what-living-abroad-taught-me).
